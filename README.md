# Jago Material

Make surfaces with character.

Build a surface from colour, grain, texture and light. Use your own artwork to make a foil label, a leather stamp or a textured print. See how it looks under light and export the surface for another project.

## Give it a try

Install with Jago Suite, or extract the portable download and open **Jago-Material.exe**.

Try a material, bring in your artwork, and adjust its placement and finish. Export a lit image for a mockup or send the surface maps to 3D Workshop.

[Find Jago Suite and downloads](https://jagosuite.co.uk/)
